NOXES / identity exploration / 01

Make complex worlds yours to shape.

A software studio for builders. The first audience is FiveM server owners and developers; the identity must keep working as NOXES grows beyond FiveM.

Promise

Make complex systems easier to shape while people stay in control.

Product truth

Understand the system. Review the proposed change. Choose what goes live.

Design rule

The mark has meaning in one color and at small sizes. No letter monogram or stock AI/gaming symbol.

Recommended route

01 — Controlled passage

Separate inputs resolve through one deliberate opening into a clear route: complexity made actionable under human control.

Strength: broad enough for every future product. Test: keep it from reading like a flowchart or a letter.

Concept route

02 — Human checkpoint

A route changes direction at a distinct control point before continuing: consequential actions remain a human decision.

Strength: directly expresses review and agency. Test: avoid resembling a generic routing or navigation icon.

Concept route

03 — Recoverable change

Two states remain connected by a clear return path: builders can move forward with confidence and recover when needed.

Strength: grounded in checkpoints and rollback. Test: avoid looking like a generic undo or infinity symbol.
