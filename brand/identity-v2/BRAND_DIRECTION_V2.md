# NOXES brand direction — concept stage

**Status:** exploratory. The symbol, palette, and message are not approved for production.

## Brand foundation

**Role:** NOXES is an independent software studio for people who build and operate digital worlds. FiveM server owners and developers are the first audience; the parent identity should also fit future, unrelated software products.

**Promise:** Make complex systems easier to shape while keeping people in control.

**Story:** NOXES builds capable tools that help builders understand what is happening, make deliberate changes, and stay responsible for what goes live. The first product applies this to FiveM servers: it reads relevant resources, stages a proposed diff, asks the owner to review it, and keeps a checkpoint for recovery. The name has no invented historical or Latin origin; its meaning comes from this mission.

**Personality:** assured, useful, inventive, and human. Speak plainly; respect the operator's expertise; avoid inflated AI claims, gamer bravado, and vague promises about future projects.

**Working message:** “Understand the system. Shape it with confidence.” Tagline options for the concept review: “Make change deliberate.” / “Build with control.” / “Tools for worlds in motion.” These are drafts, not approved copy.

## Brand architecture

- NOXES is the enduring parent identity.
- The first product is presented as **NOXES for FiveM** while its existing product name remains unchanged in the app.
- Each later product gets its own clear name and audience description, endorsed by NOXES. The identity must not rely on FiveM imagery.
- The portfolio shows launched products as active. Any real announced project is explicitly marked as in development; do not create placeholder products or imply an unannounced roadmap.

## Visual direction

- Explore a **warm signal** palette: graphite `#1D1F20`, warm white `#F4F0E8`, deep ember `#B7472F` for light surfaces, and brighter ember `#F28A62` for dark surfaces. Keep status colors independent. Verify final text and control contrast before applying tokens.
- Replace cobalt and lime as brand colors. Avoid generic neon gradients, stock circuit patterns, AI brains, shields, moons, game controllers, and mascots.
- Use a confident display grotesk, a quiet and highly legible UI sans, and a mono face only for short technical labels. Select locally loadable fonts after the concept review; do not rely on a remote font request for the identity to render.
- Keep the symbol one-color first. Color and animation may support it but must never carry its meaning.

## Symbol routes for review

All three sketches are original vector explorations, not finished logos. They are based on the chosen brand promise and do not use a letterform.

1. **Controlled passage — recommended.** Several separate inputs resolve through one deliberate opening into a clear route. It expresses complexity becoming actionable while a person controls the transition. See `concept-01-passage.svg`.
2. **Human checkpoint.** A continuous route changes direction at a distinct control point before it can proceed. It expresses the human review that precedes a consequential change. See `concept-02-checkpoint.svg`.
3. **Recoverable change.** A path connects a before-state and an after-state around a stable return point. It expresses the confidence to make changes because recovery remains possible. See `concept-03-return-path.svg`.

The concept review selects one route, or gives targeted feedback. Only then should the chosen drawing be refined into a production mark and the old brand assets replaced.

## Motion and production system

- The chosen mark's motion tells the same story: separate parts settle into a deliberate route, pause at the control point, then resolve. Use one brief reveal or a calm loop; no flashing or attention-seeking pulse.
- Ship a static vector master and PNG exports. Provide horizontal and stacked lockups, symbol-only, one-color, light/dark variants, square profiles, social/platform canvases, and desktop icon sizes. Supply a GIF for surfaces that support it; on the website use a static default and motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- After symbol approval, update the website, web app, desktop client, Canva Brand Kit, and editable social/Discord templates from one documented token set. Preserve current product behavior.
- Use Higgsfield for approved campaign imagery and motion art direction, not for final logo geometry. Do not submit new credit-bearing generations during this concept stage. Use Canva for the review presentation, approved Brand Kit, and editable platform templates.

## Review checks

- Explain the symbol's meaning in one sentence and show how it comes from the brand promise.
- Check silhouette at 16 px, 24 px, and app-icon scale; test black-only, white-only, and reversed backgrounds.
- Check that it does not read as N/U, a familiar control symbol, or a FiveM-only emblem.
- Preview website/app themes in light and dark modes, desktop and mobile; test reduced motion and platform crop-safe areas.
- Do not publish the rebrand or make social/Discord posts until the selected identity is approved. Announcements remain on-demand and use only updates supplied by the owner.
